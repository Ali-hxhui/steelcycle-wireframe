SteelCycle · wireframe · user flow

Morning dispatch list for the Oakland corridor

One user, one job. The person who opens the app is the POGOH early-shift dispatcher. The person who pays is the operations lead. The job is to decide, before 07:00, which of five stations need bikes and which need empty docks at 07:00, 08:00, and 09:00.

1. Open at 04:00Forecast is already issued. No model settings on this screen.
2. Read the listFive stations. One row is a station, an hour, a net flow, and one flag.
3. Open a station24-hour net-flow chart, 80% band, weather, and last week’s same hours.
4. Copy the rowsSelected rows go onto the drivers’ existing sheet. The app does not assign a truck.
5. After the peakDispatcher marks which flagged moves were actually made. That log is the next measurement.

What the product does

  1. Forecast dashboard. Issued at 04:00 Pittsburgh time. Window is 07:00–09:00. Target is hourly departures minus arrivals.
  2. Inventory flags. A stated 04:00 inventory (today: half of dock capacity) turns net flow into “needs bikes” or “needs empty docks.” Departure stations use the low-stock flag. Arrival stations use the full-dock flag.
  3. Station forecast. Point path plus the 80% interval, compared with last week’s same hour. The interval is visible so the dispatcher can see a spike the point forecast shrinks.
  4. Hand-off, not routing. Export or copy into the worksheet drivers already use. No route, no stop order, no campus dashboard.
What has to exist outside the app A live dock feed (GBFS) replaces the half-capacity start. Until it is connected, every flag is labeled “assumed start.” The driver’s schedule and work order stay in the system POGOH already uses. Weather forecast and the CMU/Pitt calendar are inputs to the model, not screens the dispatcher edits.
Stations in this pilot: 20 Boulevard of the Allies & Parkview, 50 Atwood & Bates, 13 S Bouquet & Sennott, 12 O’Hara & University Place, 59 Forbes at TCS Hall. Counts on the screens are one sample morning, issued at 04:00 from an assumed half-full start.
